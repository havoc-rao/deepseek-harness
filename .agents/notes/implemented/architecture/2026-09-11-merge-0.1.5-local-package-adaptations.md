# Agent Note: Local package adaptations in the 0.1.5 master merge

Status: implemented

English | [中文](2026-09-11-merge-0.1.5-local-package-adaptations.zh.md)

## Problem

Merging `offical/master` (0.1.5, 3364 commits) into the local master line (77 local commits: electron/iPad/CLI extensions and the extracted toolview packages) restructured every platform the local feature line built on: `dsh-client-runtime` was deleted (split into `dsh-client-store`, `dsh-client-ui-conversation`, `api/*-controller`), `ui-conversation`'s chat subtree moved into the new `ui-chat` package, and the theme, workspaces, and settings APIs were replaced. Git's rename detection carried official's evolution into the moved local files, which left the tree compiling nowhere: broken relative imports, dead re-exports, view-based test fixtures against args/metadata-based models, and local packages dropped from the build aggregates.

## Decision

**ui-tool-kit absorbs official's model evolution and owns the row surface.** Official continued developing `ui-tool`'s `tool/` subtree (which local commit 54a87d68ae had extracted into `ui-tool-kit`); rename detection applied that evolution to the moved copies, and the merge keeps that result. The flattened layout fixed `../models/` imports to `./models/`; the kit's `/client` entry widened to the full consumer surface (`readCallLine`, `formatToolBody`, terminal predicates, `singleResultText`, `imageCardModel`, and friends). Two local extensions: `ToolRow.summarySuffix` widened from `string` to `ReactNode` (the file-mutation chip is a JSX tree; `''` suppresses the row's own plain `+A -R` diffStat), and the `tool.call.images` slot declaration moved from ui-tool's contract into the kit (`src/client/image-slot.ts`) — the kit's `ToolRow` dispatches that slot and cannot see ui-tool's declaration merge, since ui-tool depends on the kit.

**ui-tool drops its own edit/write rows.** Official's `toolviews/file-mutation-row.tsx` registered the same `tool.call.toolview` keys as the local `ui-toolview-file-mutation` package; keeping both would double-register `edit`/`write`. The local package (with its colored `+A -R` chip) stays the single registrant, and the web-app bundle composition lists `ui-tool-kit` and `ui-toolview-file-mutation` again. Official's tests in `ui-tool/tests` import kit models through the public `/client` entry.

**ui-paper owns its whole contract on the official theme seam.** The old API (`registerPaperToneLayers`, `setPaper`, `snapshot.paper`, theme-owned `ui-theme.paper` settings field) no longer exists. The plugin now owns the vocabulary, schema, and `ui-paper` settings namespace (`src/paper-settings.ts`), contributes the active tone's layer through `ctx.theme.overrideTokens('ui-paper', …)` (re-calling replaces; the effect teardown holds the latest disposer), adopts the durable preference through its own bound scope, and registers its settings row after the official font-size row (`order: 12`). The host half reads the tone from its own namespace and the theme preference from the theme namespace.

**The message-reference slot survives as a chain over `projectUserText`.** Official moved inline reference rendering into the `projectUserText` primitive; the local extensible slot is rebuilt on top of it: the primitive accepts `renderReference?: (owner) => ReactNode | undefined` (undefined declines and keeps the shipped chip), `ui-chat` declares `conversation.message.reference` as a chain slot and threads it through `ChatView`/`ChatNodeSeat`/`MessageItem`. The default chip is the primitive's own arm, not a chain occupant, so extensions register with selectors and decline token kinds they do not own.

**workspace-logo is abandoned** (product decision): its platform (apiproxy host, runtime workspaces `setLogo`, `WorkspaceView.logo`, three `sidebar.workspaces.*` slots, the `IconCameraOutline16` glyph, and the HoverCard `width` extension) had no successor in the official tree, and a full port onto the new workspace-controller wire API was out of scope for this merge. The package, its fixtures, its icon, and its e2e additions are deleted; the workspace-management e2e is back to the official expectations.

**Local aggregates restored.** Official's `tsconfig.client.json`/`tsconfig.host.json` dropped the local-only packages (`ui-paper`, `ui-tool-kit`, `ui-toolview-file-mutation`, `apps/electron`); the merge re-adds them (TS6307 otherwise). `native/landlock-run` was NOT restored — its source is gone (the directory only held stale build output).

**Local features re-targeted onto evolved APIs:** the HMR host-restart handshake regained `hostInstance()` on the official modules registry; `healProfilesModuleFallback` calls pass the options object; the hooks bridge's elicitation observer rides the `user-questions/request` waterfall (prepend + `next()`) instead of the deleted `userQuestions/ask` event; `apps/cli` passes `ProfileTemplate`'s `bundles`/`patchReload` explicitly; the stale `websocket-downlink` re-export is gone with its last consumer.

## Consequences

Local feature packages are the adaptation boundary: everything official restructured is adopted wholesale, and the local line's product features re-target the new seams rather than forking platform code. The kit's ReactNode `summarySuffix` and its `tool.call.images` declaration are the two deliberate divergences from official's `ToolRow`; future official merges will surface them as small conflicts there instead of silent drift. The workspace-logo deletion is recorded here so the feature's design (host-record persistence, hover-card presentation) is findable from history if it is ever rebuilt on the workspace-controller API.
